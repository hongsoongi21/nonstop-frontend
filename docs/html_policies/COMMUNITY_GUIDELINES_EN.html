Nonstop Community Guidelines

Effective Date: January 21, 2026

Nonstop is a space where university students freely communicate and share information. To ensure a pleasant and safe experience for all members, we operate according to the following guidelines. Violation of these guidelines may lead to service restrictions in accordance with our Terms of Service.

1. Core Principles

  • Mutual Respect: All members must respect each other's dignity. Acts of hurting others behind anonymity are strictly prohibited.
  • Academic Integrity: As a university community, we adhere to academic ethics and discourage acts that promote academic misconduct.
  • Compliance with Laws: Members must comply with all Uzbek laws and university regulations.

2. Prohibited Acts

The following types of posts (articles, comments, photos, messages, etc.) may be deleted without notice, and the author may face suspension.

① Attacks on Others and Causing Discomfort

  • Profanity, defamation, and insulting remarks towards specific individuals.
  • Discriminatory or hate speech based on race, gender, religion, region of origin, or disability.
  • Doxxing: Revealing another person's personal information, such as real name, contact information, or photos, without consent.

② Uzbekistan-Specific Prohibitions (Extremely Important)

  • Political/Religious Incitement: Provocative posts that threaten national security or incite political/religious conflict.
  • Disturbing Public Order: Spreading false information that may cause social chaos or plotting illegal gatherings.

③ Academic and School-Related Misconduct

  • Requesting or plotting academic dishonesty such as proxy attendance, proxy exams, or assignment substitutes.
  • Unauthorized sale of past exam papers and sharing of copyright-infringing materials.
  • Spreading unverified rumors or unfounded defamation against the university or faculty.

④ Commercial Promotion and Spam

  • Commercial advertisements without permission from the administration (tutoring, self-employment promotion, loans, etc.).
  • Spamming by repeatedly posting the same content.
  • Sending indiscriminate promotional messages to other users.

⑤ Respect for Life and Safety

  • Self-harm/Suicide: Posts that threaten the life of oneself or others or imply/encourage self-harm. In case of an emergency, the administration may report to the relevant authorities.
  • Violence and Crime: Posts that plot crimes or glorify violence.

⑥ Impersonation and Illegal Transactions

  • Impersonation: Acting by pretending to be an administrator, school official, or representative of a specific organization.
  • Illegal Transactions: Buying and selling items restricted by law, such as alcohol, tobacco, drugs, medicine, or illegal recordings.

⑦ Prohibition of Unauthorized External Leakage

Any responsibility arising from capturing and distributing posts, comments, or messages within the service to the outside (SNS, other communities, etc.) without the author's consent lies with the person who distributed it.

3. Reporting and Ban System

Nonstop operates a user-report-based management system for self-regulation.

  • Automatic Hiding: If a post receives more than a certain number of reports (e.g., 3 reports), it will be automatically hidden from the list.
  • Admin Review: Reported posts are finally judged by the administration for guideline violations.
  • Suspension Levels:
    • Warning: Cautionary measures for minor violations.
    • Temporary Ban (3 / 7 / 30 days): For repeated violations or serious negligence.
    • Permanent Ban: For irreparable violations such as criminal acts, habitual rule violations, or selling accounts.

4. Content License and Copyright

  1. The copyright of the posts published by members belongs to the members themselves.
  2. By using this service, members agree that the Company may use the posted content for search exposure within the service, service promotion (SNS, etc.), and statistical data free of charge. (Note: Personally identifiable information such as nicknames will not be exposed.)
  3. Upon receiving a copyright infringement report (e.g., DMCA), the Company may immediately hide the post and provide the author with an opportunity to explain.

5. Anonymity and Post Retention Policy

Upon account deletion, personal information is destroyed, but anonymous posts and comments are not automatically deleted to maintain the context of the community. If you wish to delete your posts, please delete them yourself before withdrawing.
