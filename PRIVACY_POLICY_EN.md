# Nonstop Privacy Policy

**Announcement Date**: January 21, 2026
**Effective Date**: January 21, 2026

'Nonstop' (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") complies with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" (ZRU-547) and related regulations. We are committed to protecting our users' personal information and handling any related grievances promptly and smoothly.

## Article 1 (Items of Personal Information Collected)
The "Company" collects the following personal information for membership registration, customer consultation, and service provision.
1. **At registration**: Email address, password, nickname.
2. **At student verification**: University name, major, student ID number, photo of student ID. (Note: Student ID photos are destroyed within 24 hours after verification is complete.)
3. **During service use**: Device ID, FCM tokens for push notifications, access logs, IP address, cookies, service usage records.

## Article 2 (Purpose of Collection and Use)
The "Company" uses the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Service Provision and Management**: Granting access to the university-only community, processing student verification.
2. **Member Management**: Identity verification, prevention of duplicate sign-ups, and prevention of fraudulent use by malicious members.
3. **Marketing and Advertising (Optional)**: Development of new services, providing event and promotional information.

## Article 3 (Retention and Use Period)
1. The "Company" retains and uses the user's personal information **until the user withdraws from the membership**.
2. However, if it is necessary to preserve it in accordance with relevant laws, it will be kept for a certain period as follows:
   - Service access records: 3 months (Based on Uzbekistan's ICT regulations).
   - Records of fraudulent use: 1 year (To prevent duplicate sign-ups and respond to violations of terms).

## Article 4 (Destruction Procedure and Method)
1. The "Company" destroys personal information without delay when it becomes unnecessary, such as the expiration of the retention period or achievement of the processing purpose.
2. Information in the form of electronic files is deleted using technical methods that cannot reproduce the records.

## Article 5 (Cross-border Transfer and Local Storage)
1. **Consent for Cross-border Transfer**: For the stable provision of services, the "Company" may use global cloud servers (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud, etc.), and in this process, personal information may be transmitted and stored in countries other than Uzbekistan. Users are deemed to have consented to this upon registration.
2. **Security Measures**: Data transferred abroad is encrypted, and the "Company" strives to maintain the security level required by local laws.

## Article 6 (Rights and Obligations of Users)
1. Users can view or modify their personal information at any time and can withdraw their consent for the use of personal information by deleting their account.
2. Users must keep their personal information up to date and may be held legally liable if they sign up using another person's information.

## Article 7 (Measures to Ensure Safety)
The "Company" takes the following measures to ensure the safety of personal information:
1. **Hash Encryption**: Passwords are encrypted with a one-way hash function, so even the company cannot read them.
2. **Transport Encryption**: All data transmissions use SSL/TLS encrypted communication.
3. **Access Restriction**: We limit the number of personnel handling personal information to a minimum and conduct periodic training.

## Article 8 (Data Protection Officer)
For inquiries regarding personal information protection occurring during service use, please contact us below.
- **Email**: support@nonstop.com
- **Telegram**: @nonstop_support

## Article 9 (Use of Third-party Services and SDKs)
The "Company" uses the following external services for stable service provision and analysis. These service providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.
1. **Google Firebase (FCM, Analytics)**: For app analytics and sending push notifications.
2. **Sentry**: For error tracking and performance monitoring.
3. **Google/Apple OAuth**: To provide social login functionality.
